THE DETAILS MATTER
A few things
you may be wondering.
Simple answers before your first review.
Do I need technical knowledge to try it?
No. Choose the person or company example, open the report and save your notes. Advanced settings are only for authorized live pilots.
Can I check a real person right now?
The public site currently offers fictional examples. Live screening needs provider configuration and authorized access. Typing a real name into the demo will not check official lists or create a clean result.
Is VRFKYC a complete KYC platform?
No. It focuses on candidate screening and contextual evidence. Identity documents, liveness, beneficial ownership rules and a complete compliance program remain outside this release.
Can any agent connect?
The public deployment exposes stateless MCP over HTTP, OpenAPI and discovery files. Compatibility depends on each client. Public tools provide metadata and demos; live tools require a token. OAuth and client certification are not claimed.
Are there free APIs for a commercial launch?
CSL offers free API-key access. GLEIF, Wikidata and Federal Register offer public data access subject to their policies. OpenSanctions offers initial trial credits, then paid bundles. A free endpoint does not eliminate rate limits or licensing conditions.
Do news results increase a risk score?
No. We do not generate an uncalibrated 0–100 risk score. GDELT returns contextual article links for separate review; namesakes and inaccurate reports are possible.
Do you store screening subjects?
This release does not persist queries, reports or review notes in an application database. Hosting and provider logging may still apply. Local downloads remain your responsibility; browser-held notes disappear when the tab closes.
What happens on GitHub Pages?
The static site can run fictional demos locally. GitHub Pages cannot host the MCP server or live API. Deploy the included Next.js service or Worker backend to enable those endpoints.
