# Privacy Notice

Version: 2026-10-23. Effective: 23 October 2026.

Current beta practices; live commercial processing remains disabled.

## 1. Who operates this service

VRFKYC is presented as a product of AliceLabs LLC (the “Operator”). This notice covers our hosted beta, its account database and its API/MCP tools. AliceLabs’ state of formation, postal business address and monitored privacy email have not yet been verified for this publication. They must be supplied before live commercial launch. This notice does not invent those details.

## 2. Information and purposes

The hosting gateway supplies an authenticated Site user ID and account email after you sign in with ChatGPT. We display the email from that request; we do not store the email or a VRFKYC password in our account database. We store a hash derived from the Site user ID, your selected audience, your free-plan status, the version and time of your terms acceptance, your commercial-use declaration, and per-month usage counts. This is pseudonymous account data, not anonymous data. It enables login-linked access, terms records and prevention of quota reset abuse.

## 3. Search data and reports

Names, optional country/date-of-birth fields, source responses and reviewer notes are processed to produce the requested review. The public beta uses fictional fixtures and blocks live data calls. Our application does not persist searched names, birth dates, reports or reviewer notes in its account database. Browser-held reports and notes last in your tab until discarded, and downloads stay on your device. Search POST bodies and authorization values should not be included in application logs. The hosting, identity, browser, agent and any future source provider may process data under their own policies; this notice does not promise zero logs everywhere.

## 4. Sources, providers and sharing

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The signed-in application uses ChatGPT Sites hosting with Cloudflare Worker/D1 resources and native ChatGPT identity. GitHub Pages does not receive hosted account cookies or provider API keys; sign-in opens the application as a top-level page. No advertising, marketing analytics or payment processor is integrated into the beta. A future activated query sends its necessary fields to the selected source only: CSL, OpenSanctions, GLEIF, Wikidata, Federal Register or GDELT. Source search is opt-in and does not automatically call every adapter. Provider roles, transfer locations, contracts and retention must be checked before activation. Stripe is planned, not currently receiving payments or billing information from this release. See Provider inventory.

## 5. Lawful processing and restrictions

Where applicable, account operation can be based on performance of the service agreement; proportionate security and abuse controls on legitimate interests; and mandatory legal records on legal obligations. A business customer must identify its own lawful basis, provide required notices and instructions and sign an appropriate processing agreement before live personal-data processing. A public list or news article does not eliminate privacy obligations. We do not infer criminality, verify PEP identity or make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

## 6. Retention and account deletion

Account preference and acceptance records remain until you use the account deletion control or an applicable request is resolved. Deleting the application account removes those records. The current UTC-month hashed usage counter is retained until that allowance expires to prevent deletion/re-registration from renewing free credits; expired counters become eligible for removal during subsequent normal service operations. Infrastructure backups, legal holds and identity-provider retention are separate and must be documented before commercial launch. We do not promise a seven-year archive or twelve-month search history that the software does not implement.

## 7. Your controls and rights

A signed-in account can view its email, preference and current usage; correct its audience preference; download its own account summary; and delete its application profile. Signing out or deleting VRFKYC records does not delete your ChatGPT account. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or objection, withdraw optional consent, appeal a rights decision, or complain to a competent authority. We do not sell account data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising; there is no advertising processing to opt out of here. California and other state rights apply when their legal conditions are met. Mandatory rights are not waived by our terms.

## 8. Requests, children and updates

This beta is for adults aged 18 or older using it for permitted commercial review, not children. A private, monitored privacy/support contact still needs to be configured; do not post subject information or private documents in public GitHub issues. Until that channel and company details are verified, live data and paid services remain blocked. Material terms changes require renewed recorded acceptance; privacy updates are dated and describe actual changed practices.
