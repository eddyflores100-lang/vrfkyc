# Cookie & Local Storage Notice

Version: 2026-10-23. Effective: 23 October 2026.

No advertising or analytics tracker is used by the application.

## Strictly necessary identity

Native ChatGPT sign-in is provided by the hosting/identity platform. Platform-managed sessions may use essential cookies or related browser mechanisms under its own notices. VRFKYC does not set a separate custom authentication cookie, collect passwords or maintain its own OAuth server.

## What the interface keeps

The application keeps the current report, notes and any pilot token in tab memory. It does not store an API key, quota or subject history in localStorage. Downloads are user initiated and stay on your device. The server, not browser storage, controls your monthly allowance.

## Tracking and choices

We do not implement advertising pixels, behavioral analytics, affiliate trackers or non-essential tracking cookies. A cookie banner claiming nonexistent tracking or universal consent is therefore not shown. If that changes, this notice and any required consent mechanism must change before introducing it. Browser or client integrations can have independent storage and telemetry.
