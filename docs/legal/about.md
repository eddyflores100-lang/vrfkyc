# About VRFKYC & AliceLabs

Version: 2026-10-23. Effective: 23 October 2026.

A focused beta, with capabilities you can inspect.

## Our focus

A straightforward workspace for people and small businesses exploring commercial-counterparty review, backed by structured tools for developers and agents. It helps organize candidate evidence, its source scope and your notes; it does not make the final compliance decision.

## What is available

Free accounts with five shared monthly searches, fictional person/company fixtures, local report exports, source documentation, API/MCP discovery and recorded terms acceptance. Real screening and payments are disabled pending launch requirements.

## What is not claimed

No verified identity documents, liveness, complete KYC/AML program, global PEP confirmation, beneficial-ownership computation, scheduled monitoring, production audit archive, certification, regulator endorsement, customer logos or invented performance measurements. MCP and PDF exports are useful features that competitors also offer.
