# Billing, Cancellation & Refunds

Version: 2026-10-23. Effective: 23 October 2026.

No payment or paid plan is active in this beta.

## Current free service

Personal Free, Business Free and Developer Sandbox each describe the same five-credit monthly account allowance. There is no card collection, invoice, checkout, auto-renewal or charge for a beta search. Proposed paid package volumes are planning information, not an offer to purchase.

## Quota and failed calls

Free credits reset at the first day of each UTC month and do not roll over. Successful fictional examples count. Confirmed provider failures and not_screened responses release their reservation. Deleting or re-registering a profile does not restore that month’s free allowance. If a request is interrupted, support may need to investigate whether it completed.

## Future purchase requirements

Before charging, AliceLabs must publish final prices, applicable taxes, billing frequency, plan inclusions and exclusions, whether a subscription renews, refund eligibility and a working cancellation mechanism. A versioned order and applicable consumer protections govern a future purchase; this beta page does not silently enroll anyone.

## Future cancellation and remedies

The intended monthly subscription model would allow cancellation before the next renewal with no future renewal charge; access for the already paid period and any unused credits would be stated at checkout. Duplicate or unauthorized charges, failed paid fulfillment and material billing errors require investigation and correction/refund when due. Statutory cancellation and refund rights remain applicable. No universal “no refunds” clause is introduced.

## Providers and taxes

Any source credits a customer buys directly are governed by that provider’s agreement, not this site. Stripe processing, Stripe Tax, refunds through Stripe and sales-tax collection are not implemented. Tax obligations depend on the LLC’s verified location, ownership, classification, income and customer jurisdictions; we publish no invented flat tax amount.
