# Data Processing Addendum — Draft

Version: 2026-10-23. Effective: 23 October 2026.

Review template only. Not executed, not a live enterprise agreement.

## Status and parties

This addendum requires a verified AliceLabs LLC legal entity/address/contact and a named business Customer, signed order, effective date and processing annex. It does not become an executed DPA merely by browsing, logging in or downloading it. Live customer personal-data processing stays disabled until the relevant terms and facts are completed.

## Roles and instructions

For Customer-instructed live queries, the Customer generally determines purpose and lawful basis and AliceLabs processes only documented instructions under the agreed service scope. AliceLabs may act as an independent controller for proportionate account operation, security and mandatory legal records. Third-party public-data providers may have independent-controller roles that must be separately described.

## Annex A — proposed processing

Purpose: source-scoped commercial counterparty candidate review. Data subjects: submitted commercial counterparties and Customer reviewers. Categories: minimum names, optional country/birth-date identifiers, source candidate evidence and reviewer information. Duration: transient query processing unless a separately agreed archive exists. Excluded: identity-document images, SSNs, payment-account details, biometric templates and unrelated sensitive data. The beta does not implement persistent report archives.

## Customer obligations

Establish and document a lawful basis and notices; verify authority and minimization; avoid prohibited consumer eligibility purposes; maintain a qualified review process; honor subject rights and source restrictions. A match is not proof of identity, criminality or full sanctions exposure. Customer instructions cannot require unlawful processing.

## Processor obligations

Use documented instructions only; restrict access to authorized personnel with confidentiality duties; apply proportionate documented security; avoid using Customer query data for advertising or unrelated model training; assist rights, security incidents and legally required assessments within an agreed scope; inform Customer of unlawful instructions; bind actual subprocessors to required protections where applicable.

## Transfers, incidents and assistance

Before execution, identify actual processing countries and adopt appropriate transfer terms, including relevant EU/UK clauses where required. No “GDPR compliant” badge substitutes for those agreements. An agreed incident procedure must provide notice without undue delay after awareness and sufficient information and updates under applicable law; no unsupported fixed 24/72-hour contractual SLA is promised by this draft.

## Deletion, audit and completion

Define deletion/return at termination, limited necessary retention, hosting backups, rights-request contacts, subprocessor-change procedure, audit/evidence access and liability allocation. Annex B must describe verified organizational/technical controls; Annex C must list contracted subprocessors and locations. Complete and review these annexes before signature. This draft does not bind upstream providers or certify regulatory readiness.
